`Assets/Scenes/TestScene4_TerrianClaude.unity` (created 2026-10-21 as a copy of TestScene3_Terrian) holds the car-tuning course. It is generated by `Assets/Scripts/Editor/TestCourseBuilder.cs`, menu **Tools > MiniCarGolf > Build Test Course Scene**. Re-running replaces the `TestCourse` object and the terrain only; all sizes are constants at the top of the script. Generated assets live in `Assets/TestCourse/` (terrain data, wedge meshes, materials).

**Layout (along +Z, everything on layer 6 "Background" = the car's groundMask):**
- Terrain 240×300, flat in the middle, Perlin hills where |x| > 60.
- z=0 curbs 0.1/0.2/0.4/0.8 m (lanes x = -9/-3/3/9)
- z=30 ramps 10°/20°/35°, 6 m long (lanes x = -6/0/6); far end is a vertical drop
- z=70 jump: 20° launch ramp, 6 m gap (`JumpGap`), landing ramp
- z=110 washboard bumps (0.15 m tight, 0.3 m wide)
- z=150 dead end U, 8 m wide, 10 m deep
- Car start (0, 1.2, -45) facing +Z.

**Known issues:** the start lane x=0 drives into the 1 m gap between the -3 and +3 curbs (car is ~1.9 m wide) and gets stuck — move the start or the curbs. Jump gap suits ~20 m/s, too long at ~7 m/s. Ramps have a sharp foot (speed loss on impact); a curved transition would help. The active car in that scene is `Hummer (2) CircleColliders` (sphere wheels); `Hummer (1)` is disabled.
